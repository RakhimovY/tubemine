Flow 01

Anonymous first analysis

anon
  1. Land on /.
  2. Click Analyze a video in the hero. Page smooth-scrolls to the live demo.
  3. The demo URL is pre-filled. Click Analyze comments.
  4. Sentiment, top words, emoji, and comments table render with the example video's data.
  5. The "Sign in to save this analysis" link at the bottom of the comments table routes to /login.

Flow 02

Anonymous to signed in

anon
  1. From any page click Sign in in the top nav.
  2. Land on /login. The Google button is the only action.
  3. Click Sign in with Google. Spinner shows briefly to indicate the OAuth redirect.
  4. Land on /dashboard?welcome=true. The welcome pulse appears for ~5s then dismisses automatically.

Flow 03

Free to upgrade

free
  1. Land on /dashboard as a Free user, or open /pricing.
  2. Click Upgrade to Pro. In production this would redirect to Polar checkout.
  3. Return to /dashboard?welcome=true&tier=pro. Welcome pulse confirms the upgrade.
  4. Pro state is reflected everywhere: tier badge, monthly cap, manage subscription card on profile.
Start from dashboard Start from pricing
Flow 04

Free quota hit on the dashboard

cap-hit
  1. Open /dashboard?quota=capped. The usage card flips to destructive style with an inline error.
  2. Try to submit the Quick analyze form. The form blocks with a cap-hit error pointing to /pricing.
  3. Click Upgrade to Pro. Lands on /pricing.
Start flow
Flow 05

Anonymous quota hit on the landing demo

cap-hit
  1. Open /?demo=capped. The live demo swaps the results card for a cap-hit notice.
  2. The notice explains the 1,000-comments-per-month anonymous cap and offers Sign up free for 5x more.
  3. Click Sign up free. Routes to /login.
Start flow
Flow 06

Pro cancellation

pro
  1. Open /profile?tier=pro to land in the Pro state.
  2. Click Manage subscription in the Billing section. In production this would open the Polar customer portal.
  3. Return to /profile?canceled=true. A toast confirms the cancellation. The Plan card shows subscription ends Jun 18, 2026.
Start flow Skip to cancellation state
Flow 07

Pro revoked at period end

pro to free
  1. Land on /profile?canceled=true after a cancellation (continuing from flow 6).
  2. In production, when Polar fires period_end, tier flips Pro to Free automatically. In this prototype, use the design preview panel's Tier toggle on profile to set Tier to Free.
  3. Open /dashboard. The Free badge, 5,000-comment cap, and "Upgrade to Pro" card all appear.
